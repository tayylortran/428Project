Title: Elm Street Road Closure Notice
Source: Pinecrest Community Bulletin (synthetic sample document)

Elm Street between 4th Avenue and 7th Avenue will be closed for water main replacement
from October 5 through November 20. Detour signs will direct traffic along Oak Street.
Residents on the affected blocks will keep driveway access. Bus Route 3 will use the
Oak Street detour and skip the Elm and 5th stop during construction.
