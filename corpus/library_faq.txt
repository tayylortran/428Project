Title: Pinecrest Public Library FAQ
Source: Pinecrest Community Bulletin (synthetic sample document)

The library is open Monday through Saturday, 9am to 8pm, and closed on Sundays.
Library cards are free for Pinecrest residents. Bring a photo ID and proof of address.
You may borrow up to 15 items at a time. Books are due after 3 weeks; DVDs after 1 week.
Late fees were eliminated in 2024. Lost items are charged at replacement cost.
The second-floor study rooms can be booked for up to 2 hours per day.
