Title: Pinecrest Recycling Guide
Source: Pinecrest Community Bulletin (synthetic sample document)

Recycling is collected every other Wednesday. Place the blue bin at the curb by 7am.
Accepted items: paper, cardboard, aluminum cans, and plastic bottles labeled 1 or 2.
Not accepted: plastic bags, styrofoam, food-soiled paper, or glass.
Glass can be dropped off at the transfer station on Route 9, open Tuesday to Saturday.
