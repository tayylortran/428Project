Title: Pinecrest Farmers Market Returns for the Season
Source: Pinecrest Community Bulletin (synthetic sample document)

The Pinecrest Farmers Market runs every Saturday from May through October, 8am to 1pm,
in the lot beside City Hall. This year features 40 vendors selling produce, baked goods,
honey, and flowers. Vendors accept cash and most cards. Parking is free in the City Hall
garage on market days. Live music is scheduled for the first Saturday of each month.
